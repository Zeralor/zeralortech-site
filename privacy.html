Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how CalloutLab, a Salesforce application made by Zeralor Tech, and the website zeralortech.com handle information. In short: CalloutLab runs inside your Salesforce org and Zeralor Tech does not collect, receive or have access to your data.

Who we are

Zeralor Tech is an independent software developer based in Mexico. You can reach us at contacto@zeralortech.com.

CalloutLab

  • Runs in your org. CalloutLab is installed in your Salesforce org and runs only there and in your browser. It has no servers of its own and sends no data to Zeralor Tech.
  • Requests you send. Requests go directly from your Salesforce org (Apex mode) or from your browser (JavaScript mode) to the endpoints you enter. What those services do with the request is governed by their own policies.
  • Saved workspaces. Workspaces, folders, saved requests and environment variables are stored as records in your own Salesforce org, private to their owner by default and subject to your org's sharing settings.
  • Secrets. Passwords, tokens and API keys are never saved by CalloutLab. They live only in the page's memory while you use them, or in Named and External Credentials referenced by name. They are removed before a request is saved or exported.
  • Browser storage. CalloutLab remembers your chosen color theme in your browser's local storage. The request history is kept in memory only and disappears when you close the page.
  • Logs. CalloutLab does not write to Salesforce debug logs. Salesforce itself may record callout details according to your org's logging settings.
  • No tracking. CalloutLab contains no analytics, advertising or telemetry.

This website

zeralortech.com uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operation, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not sell or share them, and we will delete them on request.

Your rights

Because CalloutLab data stays in your own Salesforce org, you control it there: you can view, export or delete it at any time. For anything else, including access to or deletion of emails you sent us, contact contacto@zeralortech.com.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
