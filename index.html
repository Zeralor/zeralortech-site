CalloutLab: test any API from inside Salesforce

A free tool for Salesforce developers and admins to build, send and debug HTTP requests, right where your integration code runs.

Why CalloutLab

When a callout fails, the question is usually where it fails: the network path from Salesforce servers, a missing Remote Site, a browser CORS or CSP rule, or the API itself. CalloutLab sends the same request from Apex and from the browser, side by side, and tells you what went wrong.

Apex or browser

Run a request server-side through Apex or client-side through JavaScript, or compare both in one click.

Named Credentials

Call callout: endpoints so secrets stay in Named and External Credentials, never in plain text.

Clear diagnostics

Timeouts, blocked hosts, oversized responses and redirects come back with a plain explanation and a hint.

Security headers

Every response is checked for HSTS, CSP, CORS, cookie flags and more, with a short note on each.

cURL in and out

Paste a cURL command to fill the form, or export your request as cURL or ready-to-use Apex code.

Workspaces

Save requests in private workspaces with folders, environments and {{variables}}, and share them when you choose.

Your data stays in your org

CalloutLab runs entirely inside your Salesforce org. Requests go straight from your org or your browser to the endpoints you choose, and saved workspaces are records in your own org. Nothing is sent to Zeralor Tech. Read the privacy policy for details.
